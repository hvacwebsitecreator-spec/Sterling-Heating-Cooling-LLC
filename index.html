About Sterling Heating & Cooling LLC

Sterling Heating & Cooling LLC has been proudly serving Northern New Jersey since 2010. We are fully licensed and insured and provide professional heating, air conditioning, and refrigeration services for residential and commercial customers.

With over 30 years of hands-on HVACR experience, we believe in doing the job right, treating our customers fairly, and providing solutions that make sense for each home or business.

Our services include heating and air conditioning installation and repair, heat pumps and ductless systems, boilers, refrigeration, system upgrades, maintenance, and troubleshooting.

As a locally owned and operated company, we take pride in our reputation and the relationships we've built with our customers. Whether it's an emergency repair, replacing an aging system, or upgrading to today's high-efficiency technology, you can expect knowledgeable service, quality workmanship, and straightforward recommendations.
